Frequently Asked Questions

How long does book formatting take?

Standard formatting typically takes 3-7 business days depending on complexity and length. Rush services are available for an additional fee.

What file formats do you accept?

We accept PDF, DOC, DOCX, TXT, and RTF files. For best results, we recommend submitting your manuscript in DOC or DOCX format.

Can you format books for both print and digital?

Yes! We provide formatting optimized for print (PDF), Kindle (MOBI), and EPUB formats. Each format is specially optimized for its platform.

Do you offer revisions?

Yes, we include up to 2 rounds of revisions with every formatting package. Additional revisions can be purchased separately.

What's included in professional formatting?

Professional formatting includes: custom chapter headers, table of contents, page numbering, proper margins, font selection, paragraph spacing, and print-ready PDF generation.

How do I submit payment?

We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and bank transfers. Payment is typically required 50% upfront and 50% upon completion.
